info: 'The Composite rate of labour underutilization (LU4) indicates the proportion of the extended labour force accounted for by the unemployed, those who are underemployed in terms of hours worked, and the potential labour force. It therefore takes into account not only unemployment but also the underutilisation of the labour supply by people who would like to work longer hours and are available to do so, as well as by people who are outside the labour force but have a need for gainful employment.<br><br>The rate is calculated as a proportion of the extended labour force:<br><b>
LU4 = (unemployed + time-related underemployed + potential labour force) / (labour force + potential labour force) × 100</b>.'
