# Globale Metadaten    
indicator_name: Poverty headcount ratio at societal poverty line (fixed at a baseline)
area: Poverty
area_description: Poverty limits people’s ability to meet their basic needs and lead self-determined lives. It affects not only income, but can also manifest itself in limited access to housing, education, healthcare and social participation. Examining poverty reveals whether economic and social progress is reaching all sections of the population, thereby contributing to a more comprehensive assessment of quality of life and prosperity.

differences: The data shown represents the proportion of the population living below the national, country-specific poverty line. According to the EU Convention, this is set at 60 percent of the median net equivalent income of the country in question. The societal poverty line’ mentioned in the indicator, with a fixed baseline, refers to a World Bank definition, according to which the poverty line is stated in purchasing power adjusted 2021 U.S. dollars and is defined as $1.30 + 0.5 * median income, but is set at a minimum level of $3.00 where the median income is very low.
