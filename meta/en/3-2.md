Water supply is a public service and is usually provided by local authorities. The obligation to connect to and use the public water supply is intended to ensure a secure supply of drinking water to the population and to prevent or restrict the private use of groundwater. Over 99% of the population in Germany is connected to central drinking water distribution networks.
