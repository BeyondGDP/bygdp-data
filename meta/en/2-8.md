info:  "<ul style='padding-left: 25px;'><li>Annual mean levels of fine particulate matter are determined using a combination of modelling outputs and measurement data provided by the German Environment Agency (UBA) and the Länder. For the purposes of this indicator, only data from background monitoring stations in urban and rural areas are included – stations not directly influenced by local emission sources such as traffic or industrial facilities. This ensures a representative depiction of large-scale exposure patterns without distortion from localised hot spots of pollution.</li> <li>The legal basis for water supply in Germany is regulated by federal level in the <a href="https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/whg_2009/" target="_blank" onclick="return confirm_alert('the Federal Ministry of Justice','En');">Federal Water Resources Act</a> (Wasserhaushaltsgesetz, WHG) and at the state level in the respective <a href="https://www.saarheim.de/Gesetze_Laender/wasserg_laender.htm" target="_blank" onclick="return confirm_alert('Saarheim','En');">Water Laws of the Länder</a>. Water supply is a public service and is usually provided by local authorities. The obligation to connect to and use the public water supply is intended to ensure a secure supply of drinking water to the population and to prevent or restrict the private use of groundwater. Over 99% of the population in Germany is connected to central drinking water distribution networks.</li></ul>"
