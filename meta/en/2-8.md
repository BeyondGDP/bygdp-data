info: "<ul style='padding-left: 15px;'><li>Annual mean levels of fine particulate matter are determined using a combination of model calculations and measurement data provided by the Federal Environment Agency (UBA) and the Länder. For this indicator, only data from background monitoring stations in urban and rural areas that are not directly influenced by local emission sources such as traffic or industrial plants are used. This provides as representative a picture as possible of large-scale particulate matter pollution, without individual local pollution hotspots influencing the result.</li> <li>The legal framework for water supply in Germany is governed at federal level by the Water Resources Act (WHG) and at state level by the respective water laws of the federal states. Water supply is a public service and is generally provided by local authorities. The obligation to connect to and use the public water supply serves to ensure a reliable supply of drinking water to the population and to prevent or restrict the private use of groundwater. Over 99% of the population in Germany is connected to centralised drinking water distribution networks.</li></ul>"
